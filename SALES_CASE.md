## The Problem

Unplanned equipment failure costs 10x more than planned
maintenance. The U.S. Department of Energy reports that
predictive maintenance programs deliver:

- 10:1 return on investment
- 12% reduction in maintenance costs
- 30% reduction in downtime
- 25% reduction in spare parts inventory

Most small and mid-size manufacturers still run on calendar-based
maintenance because building a predictive model in-house takes
money and time most teams do not have.

## What Datafructus Does

A computational species that learns equipment baselines from
sensor data. When readings deviate, it alerts. When sensors
drop out, it reconstructs what it missed. It improves with each
exposure to your machines.
